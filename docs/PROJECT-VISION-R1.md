# Project Vision R1

## Core idea

`stoneage-rebuild` begins as digital archaeology and grows into a modern single-player reconstruction.

The project does not assume that the earliest StoneAge release already contained a grand, fully planned lore bible. One central research question is precisely how much of the world, setting, and systems existed at each historical stage and how much was added later.

## Historical objective

Reconstruct, as far as surviving evidence allows:

- the original JSS concept;
- the 1999 beta and launch state;
- the earliest world, villages, characters, pets, maps, systems, and text;
- the path from Japan to Taiwan and Mainland China;
- the evolution through 1.82 and later major versions;
- the first appearance of important systems and lore concepts.

## Rebuild objective

After a reliable historical baseline exists, build a modern single-player game from scratch.

The rebuilt game may ultimately include valuable content from later StoneAge versions, but systems should not be stacked mechanically. They should be introduced through coherent world development, story progression, culture, exploration, technology, ecology, and player growth.

## Emotional objective

Preserve and strengthen memorable milestones rather than trivializing them through immediate access.

A key example is riding pets: the historical fact of when riding appeared is separate from the player's emotional experience of earning the ability for the first time. The rebuild should protect such milestones.

## Design principle

Before adding something, ask two questions:

1. Is it good game design?
2. Does it belong in this world?

Both should be true.

## Non-goals for Phase 0

- Choosing a final engine.
- Writing production gameplay code.
- Recreating copyrighted art/audio assets for distribution.
- Designing every future storyline before the historical foundation is understood.
