## Evidence rule

- A modern professional profile is not contemporaneous 1999 evidence.
- A named self-authored professional profile can support a later first-person employment/project claim when the identity is independently corroborated.
- Independent credits on another JSS game can authenticate JSS staff identity and role history, but do not by themselves prove a StoneAge role.
- Generic employment duties must not be silently converted into exact StoneAge-specific authorship.
