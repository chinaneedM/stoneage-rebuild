# StoneAge 2001 Mainland Launch Physical Carrier — R1

Date: 2026-09-27  
Status: **HIGH-PRIORITY OPEN / EARLY OFFICIAL MAINLAND CARRIER**

## Historical identity

A contemporaneous Sina launch report states that the Simplified-Chinese Mainland StoneAge launched in Beijing on **2001-01-10** and identifies the distribution chain as:

- production: Japan System Supply / JSS;
- authorization: 北京华义联合软件开发有限公司;
- agency: 智冠电子（北京）有限公司;
- publication/distribution: **广西金海湾电子音像出版社**.

The same report says the Mainland launch issued **four different package variants**.

A 2002 Peking University economics paper independently describes StoneAge as JSS-developed, Beijing-Zhiguan represented, Waei-authorized, and formally issued by 广西金海湾音像出版社 on **2001-01-10**. This is independent corroboration, not a disc inventory.

## Why this route outranks the Wanfang 2.5 route

The Wanfang 2.5 disc is an exact surviving physical disc identity but remains an unclassified secondary carrier. By contrast, 广西金海湾 is directly tied by contemporaneous evidence to the **initial Mainland publication/distribution chain**.

If a publicly obtainable Guangxi-Jinhaiwan-labelled optical image or complete file tree is recovered and authenticated, it could become:

1. an early Mainland clean-client specimen;
2. a strong diff point against accepted Taiwan v1.0;
3. evidence for which resources/maps/runtime structures were present at Mainland launch;
4. a much earlier chronology anchor than 2.5/5.0 descendant media.

That makes this route higher-information even though the exact physical specimen has not yet been resolved.

## Four-package caution

The contemporaneous source says four differently packaged editions were issued. Therefore:

- one package photograph is not automatically representative of all launch boxes;
- distinct outer artwork does not prove distinct optical pressings;
- conversely, identical disc artwork must not be assumed until visible/photo/file evidence establishes it.

Each recovered package/disc needs its own carrier identity.

## Promotion gate

Before any present-day listing can become historical reconstruction evidence, recover at least one of:

- readable package front/back + disc-face photographs;
- exact ISBN/ISRC/catalogue/product number tied to the artifact;
- read-only ISO/BIN/CUE or complete file tree from a public preservation source;
- hashes and volume/filesystem metadata;
- installer/runtime metadata that can be compared to Taiwan v1.0 and later Mainland lineages.

Seller words such as `正版`, `二手`, or condition grades are not authentication.
