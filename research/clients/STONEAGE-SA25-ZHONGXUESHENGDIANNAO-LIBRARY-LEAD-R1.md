## Call-number semantic correction

The suffix must **not** be interpreted as a calendar month or 2002 issue number.

A neighboring catalogue record in the same electronic-media class uses `TP3-794/20.14` for **`《电脑迷》2009年第7月号下配刊光盘`** with publication date `2009.7`. Therefore the trailing `.14` is demonstrably a local holding/item sequence in at least that family, not “14月” or a directly encoded publication month.

Consequently:

- `TP3-794/12.2` does not mean “2002年2月”;
- `TP3-794/12.14` does not mean “第14月/第14期” without an explicit catalogue join;
- none of the observed `中学生电脑` holdings can currently be promoted to the **2002攻略特刊**.
