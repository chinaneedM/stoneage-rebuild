# StoneAge Mainland 1.0 Test Carrier & Retail Manual Print Split — R1

Date: 2026-09-27  
Status: **HIGH-PRIORITY OPEN / PRE-RETAIL CARRIER + PHYSICAL PRINT DISCRIMINATOR**

## 1. Pre-retail distribution is now evidenced

A surviving mirror of a contemporaneous China.com / 中华网游戏频道 mail-order notice for the formal 2001-01-12 release says registered users who had participated in an earlier `《石器时代》试玩版赠送活动` received a discount on the formal copy.

This establishes a pre-retail trial-copy giveaway surface before formal nationwide sale.

It does **not** establish whether the giveaway was:

- an optical disc;
- a download;
- a magazine insert;
- the same object later described by a collector.

## 2. Collector's Mainland 1.0 test-carrier claim

In the 2020 Bahamut 1.82 package article, physical-media collector `stoneage2017 / 寂寞如風` says that the Mainland 1.0 test item known to him and obtained by him was:

- a **test manual**;
- with a **disc**;
- distributed **with a magazine**;
- **without a retail box**.

This is later first-person physical-collector testimony. It is materially more specific than the generic trial-giveaway notice, but currently lacks a named magazine/issue, public disc/manual photo chain, optical metadata or hashes.

Classification: **OPEN / high-value carrier lead**.

Do not merge this claim with the China.com giveaway event unless a magazine, participant, disc or manual identifier links them.

## 3. First- vs second-print retail manual is real

A preserved transcription of Beijing Waei's 2001-03-12 apology says:

- a manual editing error caused a dispute over 50 hours;
- **second-batch newly printed manuals would be corrected**;
- **first-batch manuals would only be corrected on the website**;
- pre-deadline registrants would receive an additional **300 WGS points = 50 hours**.

Therefore a genuine first-print manual and corrected second-print manual should contain a diagnostic textual difference.

This is potentially more useful for carrier dating than outer-box artwork alone.

## 4. What was the actual misprint?

Evidence currently separates into three layers:

### FACT / corrected standard

17173's surviving StoneAge 1.0 charging page says:

- 6 points/hour;
- 300 points = 50 hours;
- 600 points = 100 hours;
- registering a purchased StoneAge product grants **300 points / 50 hours**.

### HYPOTHESIS / likely first-print statement

A 2003 17173 player retrospective says that what had previously been stated as **600 points became 300 points**.

Together with Waei's official statement that the manual error cost players 50 hours, this strongly suggests:

- erroneous first-print statement: **600 points / 100 hours**;
- corrected statement: **300 points / 50 hours**.

But the first line is **not yet FACT**. We still need the actual first-print manual page or a contemporaneous source explicitly quoting it.

### Separate launch-promotion statement

Sina's contemporaneous January launch coverage says the four Mainland packages included **45 hours** of free time.

Do not normalize 45h to 50h or 100h. It may represent a launch-promotion accounting rule or different copy-writing layer; its exact relationship to the WGS registration benefit is unresolved.

## 5. Recovery priority

The priority order is now:

1. recover the collector's Mainland 1.0 test manual/disc photo chain and identify the magazine/issue;
2. recover a first-print retail manual page showing the disputed WGS benefit;
3. recover the corrected second-print equivalent;
4. bind either manual to an exact package/disc carrier;
5. if public optical bytes emerge, immediately switch to hashes, file-tree inventory and diff against Taiwan v1.0.
