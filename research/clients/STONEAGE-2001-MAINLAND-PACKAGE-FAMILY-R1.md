# StoneAge Early Mainland 1.82 Package/Disc Family Control — R1

Date: 2026-09-27  
Status: **LATER COLLECTOR CONTROL / NOT BYTE PROVENANCE**

## Why this matters

The current high-priority Mainland recovery target is the early Beijing-Waei / Guangxi-Jinhaiwan client-carrier family. A provisional disc-face transcription is `ISBN 7-900323-57-0/TP·026`, but the full identifier is not independently confirmed.

Two later posts from the same StoneAge physical-media collector narrow the package/disc family without closing provenance.

## Package-family observation

A 2020-09-17 Bahamut collector post for the 1.82 client package says:

- the ordinary 1.82 new-user package contained the CDK on the manual back plus one installation disc;
- four common package-front variants are shown;
- the collector states that the backs of those four common packages are the same;
- a separate `上网包` variant is described as including an internet-access card and is treated separately from the four common packages.

This is later collector evidence, not contemporaneous launch documentation. It does **not** prove that the four photographed common packages are exactly the four package designs reported by Sina for the 2001-01-10 launch.

## Disc-family observation

A 2021-01-07 post by the same collector inventories StoneAge optical media and describes the familiar Beijing-Waei 1.82 client disc as the standard/unified disc used across ordinary Mainland package variants. The post separately identifies a less-common Mainland `上网包` disc.

This materially narrows the search surface:

- ordinary 1.82 package artwork may differ while sharing a common back and common disc artwork family;
- the `上网包` disc must remain a separate carrier identity;
- a readable back from any one of the four ordinary-package examples could therefore be useful as a shared-template candidate.

## Evidence boundary

Do **not** promote any of the following to FACT from these collector posts alone:

- byte identity across the four ordinary packages;
- January-2001 first-press identity;
- exact executable/client version;
- exact ISBN/ISRC/catalogue number;
- identity between the provisional `7-900323-57-0/TP·026` disc and every ordinary package;
- identity between the collector's four common packages and Sina's four launch designs.

The two posts are by the same collector and therefore are not independent corroboration of each other.

## Operational consequence

The next public-image search should prioritize:

1. high-resolution **backs** of any of the four ordinary 1.82 packages;
2. high-resolution common Beijing-Waei / Jinhaiwan 1.82 disc faces;
3. package-to-disc photo chains showing which disc came from which box;
4. independent catalogue/CIP metadata for the provisional publication number.

Treat the `上网包` as a separate branch during carrier tracking.
