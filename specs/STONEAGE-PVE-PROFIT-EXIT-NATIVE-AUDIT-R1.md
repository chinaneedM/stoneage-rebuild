## Facts established within this boundary

- The original scan follows side order and ascending entry positions. A
  player at slot0/4 is processed before its paired pet at5/9 on that side.
- Player ultimate invokes the exact selected helper, then exits the player.
  Its separate paired cleanup and carried non-mail HP/status restoration can
  remove/heal a still-unprocessed dead pet. That pet receives no additional
  death count/penalty in the same scan. No selection still allows paired cleanup.
- If a pet ultimate was processed on an earlier profit call, DEFAULTPET is
  already cleared before later player death/ultimate reads. Both operations
  preserve the owned-pet pointers. A nonselected pet ultimate also clears it.
- HP-positive entries with ultimate flags, and dead entries already marked
  ISDIE, do not trigger another scan death. Normal and ultimate enemy kills
  retain pending earned EXP and cannot duplicate it on a repeated scan.
- All ten original unconditional statuses and ISDIE are cleared by the player
  exit's original BadStatusAllClr calls; pet-only Exit does not invent that clear.
